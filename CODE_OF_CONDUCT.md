# Code of conduct

This project serves travellers and the people who live in the places they visit. We expect the same respect in
the project itself: in code review, issues, discussion channels and on the platform.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and assume good faith. Contributors range from first-time editors to experienced developers and
  write in many languages.
- Disagree about facts with evidence, not with people.
- Respect local knowledge and local communities; describe places and people fairly.
- Declare conflicts of interest (for example, owning a business you are writing about).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discrimination based on caste, religion, ethnicity, region, language, gender, sexual
  orientation, disability, age or any other identity.
- Deliberately false or misleading contributions, undeclared self-promotion, review manipulation or spam.
- Publishing other people’s personal information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or moderation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers and city stewards may remove content, reject or revert contributions, and suspend accounts that
violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact
details) or with the in-product “Report” action. Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters will not face
retaliation.
